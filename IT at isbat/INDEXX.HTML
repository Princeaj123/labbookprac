Welcome to the IT Department

The Department of Information Technology at ISBAT University prepares students with practical, industry-relevant computing skills. Our lecturers combine academic knowledge with real-world experience.

Students learn in modern laboratories and work on hands-on projects, graduating ready to build careers in Uganda's growing digital economy and beyond.

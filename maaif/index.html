Overview

The Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026 is organised by the Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Industry and Fisheries (MAAIF) to showcase Uganda's coffee sector to the world. Over three days, farmers, processors, exporters, roasters, buyers and policy makers will meet to trade, learn and build partnerships along the whole coffee value chain.

Visitors can explore exhibition booths, attend expert-led sessions on productivity, quality, climate resilience and value addition, and taste some of the finest Robusta and Arabica coffees grown in Uganda. Join us at Speke Resort Munyonyo and be part of the growth of Uganda's most important export crop.
